## 🤖 Coach Reflection

July is the month the running journey went from "can I complete this distance?" to "how far can I comfortably build?" Every rung of the 6K → 10K ladder was cleared, structured speed work entered the plan for the first time, weight dropped below 90 KG, and the month closed with a first 12K. The standout physiological signal is that this enormous jump in distance came with stable low-150s heart rates on easy efforts — endurance, not just willpower, is carrying the extra kilometers.

The two things to carry forward are logistical rather than physiological: make hydration automatic for long runs, and keep long runs easy enough to stay aerobic. The self-chosen break after the 10K was a mature call and a good template for managing the transition into Half Marathon volume.

No injury or overtraining risk is indicated by the logged data; the discomfort episodes were transient and self-resolved.

**Recommendation for August:** consolidate rather than chase new distance records every week — hold the aerobic base, keep one long run and one quality session per week, default to carrying enough water on anything over 8 KM, and use the improving HR-at-pace trend as the cue for the next volume step toward the Amartha Half Marathon.
