# 🏃 Running Log — September 2, 2026 (Evening)

## 📋 Workout Summary

A huge milestone: the **first-ever 15 KM long run**, completed in the evening in **02:15:57** at a **9:01/km** pace.

This is a new **longest run PB**, comfortably clearing the previous best of 12.31 KM (2026-08-07) and hitting the 15 KM long-run target directly.

The run held together well for most of the distance, but leg pain set in around **km 14** and stayed through to the finish. Despite that, the overall feeling was happiness and pride at completing it. This was also the first time trying an **energy gel** during a run.

## 😊 Reflection

This was a genuinely proud moment — the first time ever running 15 KM, and by a wide margin the longest run to date.

The run felt manageable through most of the distance, but leg pain started around km 14 and continued through the final stretch. Pushing through that last kilometer-plus with the leg already hurting was tough, but the overall feeling afterward was happiness and pride rather than discouragement.

Trying an energy gel for the first time during this run was also a meaningful experiment — worth paying attention to how the body responded to it, both during the run and in recovery afterward.

This run directly follows the lessons from earlier in the month: running in the evening (avoiding heat) and building up through the incomplete 16 KM attempts and duration-based runs in late August clearly paid off here.
