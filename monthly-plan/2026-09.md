## 📐 Progression Rule

Per `ai/WORKFLOW.md`, weekly mileage increases by **no more than 10% over the previous week** by default.

| Week | Target Mileage | Basis |
|---|---:|---|
| Week 1 (9/14–9/20) | **36.0 KM** | User-set target — a deliberate rebuild week, not a progression baseline |
| Week 2 (9/21–9/27) | **44.0 KM** | Explicit override — see below |
| Week 3 (9/28–10/4) | **~48.5 KM** | 44.0 × 1.10, 10% rule resumes off Week 2's actual mileage |

**Why Week 2 isn't +10%:** Week 1's 36 KM is intentionally suppressed to rebuild after the break — it's a cutback week, not the new normal. Applying the 10% rule on top of an already-reduced week would compound the setback instead of just recovering from it. So Week 2 targets 44 KM, close to the pre-break capacity (Aug 24–30 hit 40.05 KM; July's mileage PB was 44.13 KM) rather than 110% of Week 1. This is the user-override exception in `ai/WORKFLOW.md`'s progression rule, applied deliberately here.

**Week 3 resumes the normal 10% rule:** off Week 2's 44.0 KM, Week 3 targets ~48.5 KM (44.0 × 1.10 ≈ 48.4, rounded to whole-KM day targets). Note Week 3 spans into October (9/28–10/4) since it's a full 7-day training week, even though this plan was requested "through end of September" — the ISO week itself doesn't end on the 30th.
